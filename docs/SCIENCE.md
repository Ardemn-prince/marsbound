# Science and simulation

MarsBound is a fictional educational simulation. Weather is generated locally, not a live forecast. Scores, six-minute sols, resource percentages, travel distances and upgrade effects are game rules, not engineering predictions. The real MOXIE experiment was a technology demonstration, not a complete human life-support system.

Learning sources checked during development:

- [NASA: Mars facts](https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/) — thin atmosphere, cold conditions and the Martian environment.
- [NASA: MOXIE](https://www.nasa.gov/space-technology-mission-directorate/tdm/mars-oxygen-in-situ-resource-utilization-experiment-moxie/) — making oxygen from atmospheric carbon dioxide.
- [NASA: Jezero's ancient lake](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/mars-2020-perseverance/perseverance-rover/nasas-perseverance-rover-deciphers-ancient-history-of-martian-lake/) — the delta and evidence of past water.

The map is stylized rather than a geographic reconstruction. The optional in-game data log links to these public sources. No personal data or research results are transmitted to NASA.
